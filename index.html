How it works

Four steps from cupboard to cash

No spreadsheets, no copy-pasting listings, and no frantic searching when something sells.

  1. Step 1

    Photograph

    Point your camera at any item. Add more angles for a better match, or flip on Bulk to scan item after item.

  2. Step 2

    AI identifies

    Retriv names the item, grades its condition and suggests a price based on what similar items sell for, in seconds.

  3. Step 3

    Assign a box

    Optionally tag scanned items to a box, so they're easy to retrieve once they sell.

  4. Step 4

    List and retrieve

    Push to a number of online marketplaces, like eBay, in one tap, or use the easy copy and paste mechanism to manually create your marketplace listings. When it sells, a notification tells you which box to open.
