Privacy notice

How we handle your details

Last updated 3 November 2026

This notice explains what we do with the information you give us when you join Retriv's early-access list on this website. Retriv is not live yet, so this covers the early-access list only. The app will have its own privacy policy when it launches.

Who we are

Retriv is run by [legal entity name]. You can contact us about anything in this notice at hello@retriv.app.

What we collect

  • Your email address, so we can tell you when Retriv launches.
  • What best describes you, if you choose to tell us, so we know who's interested and can make the launch email relevant.
  • When you signed up.

We don't ask for anything else, and we don't use cookies or tracking on this website.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

We use your details only to email you about Retriv's launch. Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by joining the list. You can withdraw it at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing us, and we'll remove you.

We will never sell your details or use them to advertise anything other than Retriv.

Who else handles it

We keep the list in our own database, hosted by Amazon Web Services in London. We also use MailerLite, an EU-based email service, to send the launch email. They only process your details on our behalf.

This website is hosted on Amazon Web Services. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads, as it would for any website using its fonts.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until you unsubscribe, or [for up to 12 months after launch], whichever comes first. Then we delete them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the details we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete them, or withdraw your consent. Email hello@retriv.app and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your details, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
